# Act IV: The Ashen Forge

Act IV ends the story of the Ash Crown. An optional one-line Act V seed sits at the very end and can be cut without loss.

Whitecliff's fire, with the three sleeping shards in it, leaves home in the Ember Cradle that Brokka and Halda forged. The village goes dark for the first time in a thousand years. The hero walks the Wayfarers' Road across the Field of Ash, where Karthax fell, to the mountain called the Black Anvil and the forge inside it.

The act runs on light. On the Field the Cradle is the hero's only light, and the dead Wayfarers are Shrouded outside it. In the Forge the mountain breathes fire down its flues, and it grows hotter as the hero wakes its Great Bellows.

The truth recasts three acts without retconning a line:
- Arna, the first Wayfarer, lit every beacon in the North from the lantern. Every beacon was the brow-stone's fire, which is why beacons lull shards and why the shards dream together.
- Isarn's wish as a boy was 'Don't leave me alone with it'. For a hundred years the shard granted it with a voice in the flame that spoke like his father, and that voice was Karthax.
- The first boss is that father, Ivar, still standing at the Anvil Gate. He dies saying 'I never spoke to you, son.'

At the Anvil of the Crown, Karthax pours himself a body of slag. The hero must make his own hammer break the ash cages of the shards' old keepers. Then the three peoples unmake the crown together: Brokka strikes, Elati sings, the hero pours Whitecliff's fire, and Isarn carries the crown into the white fire. Every beacon in the North goes out at once. The hero carries the clean lantern home and lights a new fire by hand, and one by one, people answer.

## Story
### premise
Act III ended with three shards asleep in Whitecliff's beacon, calling to the fourth: the brow-stone in Isarn's lantern. Only the fire that made them can unmake them (d.isarn.d8), so the fire of the last lit beacon has to leave home.

Brokka came up from Deepstone, and she and Halda forged the Ember Cradle (Η Κούνια της Θράκας): an iron brazier with heat-keeping runes, carried on the hero's hip. Its glow becomes her only light.

The road north is the Wayfarers' Road. It runs from dead beacon to dead beacon across the Field of Ash, the same field as the Breaking memory (d.tear.breaking.1), to the Anvil Gate and the mountain called the Black Anvil (Το Μαύρο Αμόνι), which Karthax hollowed into his forge. Isarn comes too, because the lantern has to go into the fire and the lantern goes where he goes.

The irony the act turns on: the Wayfarers swore the crown would never go back to its forge, because only there could it be remade. Only there can it be unmade, too.

### villain
Karthax, the Ash King (Κάρθαξ, ο Άναξ της Τέφρας).

**His wish and its cage.** He forged the crown wishing 'Να μη με ξεχάσουν ποτέ' / 'Let them never forget me'. The shard rule caged him as well. Intro.3 already says the beacons were lit 'so no one would forget', and they were lit from his own brow-stone. So for a thousand years the North remembered him every night by his own fire. He was remembered and never obeyed: unable to die, a fire split across four stones.

**His method.** He never needed armies: 'Δεν κατέκτησα ποτέ κανέναν. Τους έδωσα ό,τι ζήτησαν.' / 'I never conquered anyone. I gave them what they asked for.' His will lives most strongly in the brow-stone.

**What he did a century ago.** He spoke to Ivar in Ivar's own voice. He walked Ivar to Durgan's hall, where Ivar told the king the shard could become a blade (d.isarn.b2, d.brokka.3). He walked Ivar to the heart of the First Oak, which began to smoke that night (d.isarn.c5, d.tear.sorrow.1).

**What he did three nights before the game.** He called his fire home. Every beacon was a piece of it, so they went dark one after another (intro.4, d.isarn.2). He woke his creatures to dig for the Barrow shard (d.isarn.3, 'they're digging for something'). He left one fire burning, Whitecliff's, and his voice sent Isarn there: 'Άφησα μία φωτιά αναμμένη. Η φωλιά θέλει ζέστη.' / 'I left one fire burning. A nest needs warmth.'

**In Act IV his voice stops hiding:**
- whispers through the Cradle at the waylamps, in Ivar's warm voice;
- open mockery after Ivar falls;
- a body of slag at his anvil.

### twist
**The watch was the cage.** Arna was the boy who searched the field for his brother after the battle. He is male, matching the shipped 'του Άρνα' in d.isarn.r3 and d.isarn.a1. He found the brow-stone and answered 'It will be watched' (d.tear.breaking.5). His full wish: 'Θα το φυλάμε. Δεν θα το αφήσουμε ποτέ από τα μάτια μας.' / 'We will watch it. We will never let it out of our sight.'

From the lantern he lit every beacon 'from the sea to the mountains'. Arna's hill, the first fire to answer in Act I, is the first beacon he lit. So the North's guardian fires were Karthax's own fire. That is why they lull the shards, and why 'what sleeps in one fire dreams together' (d.isarn.d7).

**What the shard made of the Wayfarers: they can never stop watching.**
- Keepers age slowly. Isarn is about 110 and looks 70.
- A keeper can set the lantern down only in another keeper's hand.
- When they hand it on they walk north and watch the Anvil Gate forever, so no fire is ever carried back to the forge. Death does not relieve them; they become the Lampless (Οι Άφεγγοι).

**Isarn's own wish.** At ten, taking the lantern from his frightened father, he said: 'Μη με αφήσεις μόνο μαζί του.' / 'Don't leave me alone with it.' The shard granted it. Every night for a hundred years a voice in the flame spoke like Ivar, and Isarn followed it through Acts I to III. Wayfarers believe the dead keepers still watch over them, so he never doubted it.

**Nothing is retconned.**
- The beacons really do lull the shards, and the hero really did save the North three times.
- Isarn's only lie is still the one he confessed in d.isarn.d7.
- Old lines read differently on replay: 'Durgan was my father's friend', 'My father went down into the Heartwood once', Amaranthe's 'I knew a beacon-keeper once' (d.lady.h2), 'something that walks beside you is listening' (d.amaranthe.die3), 'Ask the Wayfarer why his lantern never goes out' (d.amaranthe.die4), and 'Someone is putting them out' (d.isarn.3).

Isarn's own answer to that last one: 'Σου είπα πως κάποιος τις σβήνει. Ποτέ δεν ρώτησα τι περπατούσε δίπλα μου.' / 'I told you someone was putting them out. I never asked what was walking beside me.'

### ending
Act IV is the finale.

**Karthax falls.** He kneels on the anvil: 'Ποιος... ποιος θα με θυμάται;' / 'Who... who will remember me?' The fires he drank spill back out of him, and the Cradle catches again. The crown lies whole on the anvil, cracked and still burning.

**The Unmaking.**
- Brokka strikes the brow-stone: 'Ο βασιλιάς μου ευχήθηκε να γίνει ο σιδεράς που θα το λιώσει. Ας γίνω εγώ. Χωρίς ευχή.' / 'My king wished to be the smith who would unmake it. Let it be me. Without a wish.'
- Elati sings the children's song from the Amber Tear (d.tear.m2.2): 'Πέσε, φύλλο, πέσε...' / 'Fall, leaf, fall...'
- The hero pours Whitecliff's fire from the Cradle into the Forge Mouth, and it burns white.
- Someone must carry the crown in, and whoever carries it burns. Isarn lifts it: 'Δεν έχει τίποτα να μου δώσει πια. Μου έδωσε ήδη τον πατέρα μου.' / 'It has nothing left to give me. It already gave me my father.'
- He hands the hero the lantern, clean now with only Arna's plain flame in it: 'Άναψε τη φρυκτωρία με το δικό σου χέρι. Και μην πεις ποτέ το όνομά του σε κανένα παιδί. Έτσι πεθαίνουν οι τύραννοι.' / 'Light the beacon with your own hand. And never tell his name to any child. That is how tyrants die.'
- His last word is the one he always gave the hero: 'Ξεκουράσου.' / 'Rest.' It answers his first, 'So you're awake'.
- He walks into the white fire. The Lampless follow with their lanterns lit at last, and Ivar takes his hand.
- From the fire: 'Σβήνω... Κάνει κρύο. Το είχα ξεχάσει.' / 'I am going out... It is cold. I had forgotten.' This echoes Durgan's 'Cold... at last'.

**The Night Without Fires.** Every beacon in the North goes out at once (Η Νύχτα Χωρίς Φωτιές).

**Quest 22.**
- The hero walks home under stars.
- At Whitecliff she lights the beacon from Isarn's lantern. It burns white-gold, and the lantern goes out quietly, as if falling asleep.
- Fires answer one by one, lit by hands: villagers on Arna's hill, Brokka at Deepstone, Elati at the beacon-tree.
- Isarn's staff stays planted by the beacon with the dark lantern hanging from it.
- Act panel: 'Πράξη IV — Ολοκληρώθηκε' / 'Act IV — Complete', sub 'Οι φωτιές ανάβουν ξανά, από ανθρώπινα χέρια' / 'The fires are lit again, by human hands'.

**Post-game.** The Shadow Gates, opened now from Isarn's staff, plus echo refights of Ivar and Karthax.

**Optional Act V seed** (one FAR_BEACONS entry; delete it to cut): last of all, a fourth fire lights far to the north on the Dark Beacon of the Field of Ash. 'Κανείς που ξέρουμε δεν την άναψε.' / 'No one we know lit that one.'

## Mechanics
### Light and Shroud, with the Ember Cradle (Φως και Σκιά, η Κούνια της Θράκας): Field of Ash and both boss fights
**The Cradle.** The hero carries Whitecliff's fire on her hip in the Ember Cradle. It lights a 6 m ring around her, drawn as a warm ground ring mesh, not only a PointLight, because R.sources caps lights at low quality.

**Other light:**
- **Waylamps:** touch to light. Each is a permanent 8 m pool, a respawn checkpoint and, for the five story lamps, a memory.
- **Braziers:** 6 m.
- **Fire areas:** the Mage's fire, the Ash-Fallen bowmen's fire arrows and burning patches.
- **Ashwing rib-coals:** 4 m for 10 s.
- **Isarn's clean lantern:** 6 m, Karthax phase 3 only.

**The Cradle drinks fire.** Any fire area inside its ring streams visibly into it (a lanternLean-style particle stream) and burns out twice as fast, and the ring swells to 9 m for 8 s. The enemies' fire becomes a risk-and-reward fuel. It is also how the player learns, before Isarn says it, that fire lit from the lantern drinks.

**Shroud.** Only the Lampless, and Ivar and Karthax in their dark phases, are Shrouded: 30% damage taken outside light, with a smoky desaturated look and a 'Σκιασμένος / Shrouded' hit text. A hit landed inside light leaves them Revealed for 3 s. Every other enemy takes full damage everywhere.

**Seen.** The Lampless alarm and Ivar's Lantern Sweep mark the hero Seen: +15% damage taken for 6 s, shown as an eye icon.

There is no drain meter, no escort and no timer.

- **Snuffed state** is runtime-only, so on load every lit lamp is lit.

### The Crown's Offers (Οι Προσφορές του Στέμματος): the Altars of the Wish, Field of Ash
The rule of the shards, made playable and kept small. At three optional altars the sleeping shards speak in their last keepers' voices and offer a gift with a cage attached. The player picks Accept (Δέξου) or Refuse (Αρνήσου).

**The gifts** (stat lines only, no death-saves, no potion rules):
- **The Throne (Ο Θρόνος),** Barrow Lord: +25% armour, +10% life. Cage: −12% movement.
- **The Forge (Το Καμίνι),** Durgan: +20% damage, +15% critical damage. Cage: −20% armour.
- **The Unfading (Το Αμάραντο),** Amaranthe: +15 life on hit, +15 life per second. Cage: −15% attack speed, the stillness of the wood.

**Consequences.**
- Gifts last until Karthax's phase 2, when he takes them back ('I lent to you. Now I take it back.').
- They decide which cage statues rise whole (two hammer blows) or cracked (one).
- A hero who refused all three is Unbound (Αδέσμευτος / Αδέσμευτη). The Act IV blessing is worth x1.5, and Isarn says «Το κουβάλησες, κι ποτέ δεν σε κράτησε.» / 'You carried it, and it never held you.'

### The Forge's Breath and the Forge Heat (Η Ανάσα και η Θέρμη του Καμινιού): Ashen Forge
The banked forge breathes.

**The breath.** Each flue (a straight Breath Gallery with alcoves and pillars) has a cycle:
- A 2.0 s inhale: a deep inhale sound, the floor grates glowing, a teleLine down the whole flue, and a red edge pulse on the HUD if the hero is inside it.
- Then 1.2 s of fire roaring down the line, which hits once per breath for 20% of max life plus burn. That is 12% on the Wanderer difficulty.
- Cover is anything solid between the vent mouth and the hero: an alcove, a pillar, a side passage.
- Fireproof monsters ignore it, and the rest take 25%.

**Cycle timing.**
- The base period is 10 s.
- A Hammerhorn pumping its station sets that flue to 5 s.
- Each Forge Heat stage shortens every period by 1.5 s, to a floor of 4.5 s.
- The 2.0 s inhale is never shortened.

**Waking the Great Bellows (q20).**
- In each bellows chamber, touching the bellows starts Brokka working it. The hero holds the room for 20 s against two waves from two doors (Ashsmiths and Ash-Fallen, Ember Ticks), while up to 2 Smoke-eaters crawl straight for the bellows' fire-pit.
- If the fire is eaten, the timer pauses until the hero relights it.
- Each completed breath raises the Forge Heat one stage (0 to 3): lava brighter, fog warmer, a music layer joins, and that chamber's vents start erupting.
- The third breath melts the slag plug on the Great Stair.
- The mountain grows more dangerous the more the hero succeeds: Karthax's will waking.

The music's rhythm section is locked to the global breath, so the beat can be learned by ear.

## Music
Five new procedural THEMES in audio.js, plus one motif.

**The lantern motif.** Four notes (A, C, B, E) on a soft reed or flute voice. It sounds:
- when a waylamp is lit;
- when Ivar remembers his son;
- when Isarn opens the clean lantern.
In the finale it resolves to D major.

**'ashfield'.** About 60 bpm, D Phrygian.
- A low bowed drone and an organ pad (D against E-flat), with filtered-noise ash wind.
- A distant two-note horn call, a falling fourth, every 20 to 30 s: the Lampless horn.
- No drums, except a slow frame-drum heartbeat that fades in while the hero is Seen.
- Lighting a waylamp blooms a warm major-third chord on the next bar.

**'ivar'.**
- Phase 1: the horn call becomes the bass ostinato.
- Phase 2: strips to drone plus heartbeat.
- Phase 3: brings the lantern motif in under the fight.

**'forge'.** 6/8 at 84 bpm, C Phrygian dominant, so it is set apart from the Halls' E Phrygian. The rhythm section is the breath, locked to forge.js MOOD.breath:
- A filtered-noise inhale swell on the 2 s telegraph, ducking the bus so the inhale is always heard.
- An exhale roar, with FM anvil clangs on 1 and 4.
Each Forge Heat stage adds a layer: brass drone, then anvil ostinato, then a low formant 'ah' choir.

**'karthax'.** 6/8 at 96 bpm, with tritone bass and full choir.
- Phase 2: fragments of the crypt, halls and Amaranthe motifs surface each time a cage statue breaks.
- Phase 3: drops everything to one held low note, and the lantern motif plays alone on a pure sine and flute over the dark.

**'newfire'** replaces 'victory' for q22: the lantern motif, harmonised and slow, quoting the town melody as each far fire answers.

**Ambients:**
- 'ashwind': gusts, and faint chimes of the hanging lanterns in the Graves.
- 'forge': bellows breath, a distant hammer, lava.

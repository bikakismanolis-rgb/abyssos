# Co-op: questions for the owner

Each has the default the code uses now; a different answer is a small change.

1. **Quest credit for guests.** Default: none. The story is the host's; a guest keeps all its XP, gold, items, levels and kills, but its own quest, story flags, waypoints and difficulty stay where they were (so no save can be broken by someone else's story). An alternative: a guest that has already reached the same quest gets it advanced with the host. Say if you want that.
2. **Guests and the story's things.** Default: only the host lights lamps, talks to the story's people, takes exits and waypoints; a guest has its own chests, shrines, stash and the shops. Should a guest be able to light a lamp or a sea-light for the host's story?
3. **Downed time.** Default: 30 s to be raised (2.5 s standing by), then up at the checkpoint at full life; raised at 40% life.
4. **How much harder, how much richer.** The table in `README.md` (data.js `COOP`). Two players: monsters ×1.7 life, packs ×1.25, XP ×1.3, magic find +25. Tell us after playing if it feels too easy or too hard; the numbers are in one place.
5. **Who may join on claude.ai.** People you share the game with at Contributor or Editor access. Viewers and Commenters play alone (the room does not let them send).

## What I built

### src/game/ai.js (the Act V block after sunkTick, "Act V: the Skotos's creatures and the coast's beasts")
- Palette: `SEA` 0x30e0a8 (sea-green: pulls and reels), `SONG` 0xbfe8ff (the song), `ICE_W` (white: ice about to go);
  red/orange for harm as everywhere.
- **Water** (`waterTick`, every Act V actor each frame): `a.swim` = def.swim or a floater (not bats/bosses) in a zone with
  water; swimmers use `swimCollide` (walls only, never pushed out of a closed water cell) and `seek` goes straight over
  water (`map.los`) and, stuck out in the water, makes for the nearest shore. `SWIM_Y`: in deep water the Sunken stand
  chest-deep (y -0.95), lice -0.3, a Reefback -0.5, with a wake behind them. Tideborne: `a.waterK` 1.3 in shallows/brine/
  deep, 0.85 on a tidal flat the tide has left, +2%/s life in the shallows; a Reefback 1.3 in any water. (combat.js
  `moveMul` multiplies `a.waterK`.)
- **tideWake** (the dormant branch of updateActors, restructured as roused / called / tide): `tideWakes(a)` every 0.25 s,
  the cell under it not dry and (for the unlit) not in lamp light. Woken by the sea they rise **calm** (`a.tideRisen`, not
  aggro; pack-mates woken through unmask's wakeIn stay calm too: `a.wakeCalm`); the hero's approach or a blow makes them
  aggro as before. `goRest`: at the ebb or low water, calm risers walk home and `lieDown` (actors.js) under their kelp /
  as a rock again at full life. Same for the Reefback (`def.wake.tide`).
- **Frozen crews** (`statueTick`, `breakStatue`): ice statues until a stage-1 crack in the 3 x 3 round one, the hero within
  4 m, or a blow; then the whole rail breaks out 0.1-0.7 s apart (ice shards, iceCrack2, their own tint back).
- **Pulls** (`pullHero(x, z, dist, dur, key, stop)`): pl.pull toward the puller, never past it, stopped by `map.castT` at
  the first deep cell or wall; hit text hud.hooked / hud.dragged.
- **Sunken** (melee): every `hookEvery` (3) blow a sea-green teleCone 2.4 m 0.6 s, Melee_Hook; a hit (n > 0) pulls 1.5 m
  (`hookStrike`). hitHero now returns the damage dealt.
- **Harpooner** (`AI.ranged` -> `harpooner`): keeps off (backs under 4.5 m), throws (OverhandThrow, release 0.47 s);
  every third throw the **reel**: 0.9 s tell (KK_Throw timed to release at 0.9 s, the coil glints, harpoonReel creak,
  sea-green teleLine 11 x 1.0), a 24 m/s harpoon; on a hit 0.9x and a 4 m pull over 0.4 s (2 m if she stands in lamp
  light); a roll during the tell (`pl.dodgeStamp`) lets it fly through her. A miss sticks in the ground 1 s (thin ice +1
  stage r 1), splashes in water, sparks on a wall. A rope (`rope`, `ropeTo`, `ropeTick`; a thin cylinder) runs from the
  hand bone to the harpoon while it flies and to her while she is reeled; ROPES map cleans up orphans and on zoneEnter.
- **Ice Singer** (`AI.singer`): caster spacing (8-11 m, flees under 5); the **song** (`songTick`): 3 s channel, one singer
  at a time per zone (`songToken`), rings round her and a wavering song-line (bolt) to the hero; at 2.0 s it locks on
  her footing with a 1 s teleCircle (white r 2.5 on thin ice / pale r 4 elsewhere); at 3 s: thin ice +2 stages r 2.5, or
  a 'brine' pool r 4 for 8 s; **fizzles in lamp light** (puff, tally songFizzled). Broken (`songBreak`) by a blow of 6% of
  her life in one frame, a stun/freeze (stun branch) or a beam (unlit stun). Emits bus `'iceSong'` (a, {x, z, ice}).
  **Wail** under 3.2 m: orange teleCone 4 m 0.5 s, 0.6x, 30% slow 2 s, Cold +10.
- **Hull-louse** (`AI.melee` -> `louse` while under/curled/bowling): waits hidden (`a.burstPack`, from actors.js) in a
  hull (gen5 `burst: 'hull'`), a crack (thin ice: the 3 x 3 cracked to stage 1 on first sight, to 2 when they burst), or a
  den (a Reefback's rock, the bear's den); `boilOut` when the hero is within 6 m of the spot, a pack-mate is aggro, a
  blow, or (hulls) a flood starts; staggered 0.1-0.6 s, 'spawn' clip, louseSkitter. Hulls give up a swarm again at each
  flood start, twice a visit at most (bus tideTurn; refills the pack to its n). In lamp light only the hero's coming
  brings them out. **Curl** (combat.js `curl`/`curlHit`): at half life or any area blow, 3 s ball (dmgTaken 0.25, no
  knockback), louseCurl, the first time the toast hud.bowl. **Bowl** (`startBowl`/`bowlStep`/`louseBurst`): the hero's
  roll through a curled louse or a hero blow with knock >= 6 (the Warden's third combo, Meteor) sends it 9 m/s for 7 m:
  heroHit(1.0) knock 4 (area: it curls the lice it hits), thin ice +1 per cell, drowns in deep water, bursts on a wall or
  a big body (0.5x r 1.5, kill credited to the hero).
- **Icemaw** (`AI.lurker`): `lurkInit` takes the nearest open water within 14 m as home (else the nearest thin ice; else
  the pounce fallback); under it is hidden and untargetable, moves 7 m/s through water and thin ice only (`swimUnder`,
  leashed 15 m from home), feels the hero within 16 m; shade under the ice (`shadeTick` -> sea.js `setShade` slots 0-2,
  faded), a dark shape and bubbles over open water. A hole within 6 m of her (open, unlit, in its leash: `lurkHole`) or,
  failing that, a webbed (stage >= 2) cell within 4 m (`breachCell`/`breach`: the cell breaks, +2 r 1.5) -> tell 0.6 s
  (bubbles, bulge ring, sealBark) -> `mawUp`: surface clip, red teleLine 7 x 1.6 and a sea-green arrow back (0.7 s) ->
  lunge (5 m of body at 16 m/s, head reach 7): a hit 1.4x, Dragged 2.5 m toward the hole, and it stops and hauls back
  with her -> up (Exposed 6 s via addVuln) biting within 2.2 m (orange teleCone 0.5 s, 1.1x) -> after 3 s it backs to the
  hole and slides in tail first. Its hole refrozen or lamp-lit while it is up (`holeAt`), or a sea hole skinned by a beam /
  a Frost Nova (`seaSkinned`) -> **Stranded** (`strand`: 4 s, x1.5, 'stranded' loop, then wriggles 1.2 m/s for the
  nearest hole). Frost Nova (bus 'frostNova', skills.js) over one under holds it below 6 s. `FALLS.icemaw` (ice.js FALLS,
  set here): through the ice under it it simply goes under there.
- **Reefback** (`AI.brute` -> `reef` for a def with a guard): claw = orange teleCone 3.2 m 0.8 s, 'attack' timed to it,
  1.1x; every third a slam = teleCircle 2.4 m 1.0 s, 'slam', 1.3x + stun 0.4, thin ice +2 r 2.4. Turns at def.turn 1.8
  rad/s (turnTo reads def.turn). Guard, rock dormancy and the tide wake are the shared code.
- **Rime Bear** (`AI.charger` + `roar`): Frost Roar when she is within 5.2 m in sight (cd 9-11 s): orange teleCone 5 m
  0.6 rad 0.8 s, the 'howl' clip (the charge keeps 'rear'), then a frost-breath cone of vapour and glints, a blue ring,
  Cold +15 and a 20% slow 2 s if she is in it, thin ice +1 r 4. Breath steams from its muzzle while it hunts.
- **Skua** (`AI.bat` -> `skua` for lightShy): perched on its roost (actors.js `roost`: three per roost point, the whale's
  skull / a mast, height by a raycast `perchY`, the rest on the shingle below); the flock lifts off a beat apart when the
  hero is within 10 m (takeoff clip, y follows anim.liftK), circles her at 4.2-5.4 m (airborne: out of reach, a blob
  shadow below), crying; dives (held 'dive' loop) only at a hero out of every pool and beam (`lightAt(..., {ring:false})`),
  two at a time per zone (`diveToken`), bites Cold +3, climbs away; a dive is broken if she steps into light; in light
  they wheel wider (9.5 m). Scatter (fear 3 from beamTick, or burning oil within 6 m): up and away. A flock with no hero
  near goes home and lands. updateActors no longer runs the ground flee for lightShy.
- Stun branch: a stun/freeze breaks a song, cancels a reel/throw/wail/roar wind-up (`HELD`), and an Icemaw caught rising
  is simply up. Bats: the wall check reverts only a move *into* a wall (a skua leaving a blocked roost cell).

## Differences from the design (code is the truth; written down)
- The Rime Bear's roar plays the bear's 'howl' clip; 'rear' stays the charge's wind-up (one clip cannot be both).
- Hull-lice are x1.25 and darkened (readability on snow at the phone camera).
- Frozen crews stand on thick ice by the ship (gen5), where nothing cracks: in practice the hero's approach (4 m) or a
  blow wakes them (a Leap's stun does, so "a Leap wakes the rail" holds).
- The sunken's "walk back to a cell with bed <= 0.3" is "walk back to where it lay" (its kelp heap on the wrack line).
- The Icemaw stops on a bite and hauls back with her (the design's lunge would carry its body through her).
- The Ice Singer's song is shown with its circle only for its last second (2.0-3.0 s): a full-length circle would sit on
  the hero for 3 s; the song line and her rings carry the first two seconds.

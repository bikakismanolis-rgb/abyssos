## What I built

### src/game/cold.js (new, ~150 lines)
- `COLD = { v, state, warmT, k, dir, fbT, held }` (state 'none' | 'chilled' | 'freezing' | 'frostbite'; k = v/100; dir
  -1/0/1 for the HUD's arrow; warmT = Warmed seconds left).
- `coldAdd(v, o)`: the only way in; gains x `HAZ5[diff].cold`; nothing outside Act V zones (`G.zone.act5`), nothing while
  Warmed (`o.pierce` reaches through: the Deep Cold), nothing while dead. Negative v drains.
- `tickCold(dt)` (player.js, after tickLight): +4/s while `pl.inWater` (tide, brine, slush), +2.5/s while
  `G.zone.gust.on` out of the lee (behind a berg downwind: the blizzard blows west in fx.js; beside the ship; in a warm
  pool); -12/s in `warmAt`, -8/s in burning ground (`fireAt`), -3/s otherwise (and while Warmed).
- `warmHero(t = 4)`: -15 at once, `pl.buffs.warmed = t` (no build-up). Called by light.js beams.
- `resetCold()`: on `zoneEnter` of a non-Act-V zone and on `respawn` (bus listeners in cold.js).
- States: 50 Chilled (`coldMul` 0.92: moveMul and skills.js attackSpeed), 75 Freezing (0.85, `coldNoRegen`: player.js
  regen off), 100 Frostbite: `rootHero(1.0, { tint: 0x9ad8ff })` + 5% max life (pure) + Cold to 70. In a boss fight
  (`G.bossActor` alive): a 50% slow for 2 s instead, held (`COLD.held`, state 'frostbite') while a red/orange telegraph
  covers her (`redUnder`: fx.js FX.teles, circle/cone/line shapes).
- HUD hooks: `COLD`, bus `'cold'` (state) on each change, `'frostbite'` ('frozen' | 'slow'), `FX.chill` (breath, fx.js
  walk5). The frost overlay is story's: `fx.js frostVignette(COLD.v)`.
- `window.__act5.cold = { COLD, coldAdd, warmHero, resetCold, coldMul }` (DEV).

### src/game/light.js
- Per-zone kinds: `lightKind()` -> 'ember' | 'sea' | null from `ZONES[id].light` (read at run time; `LIGHT.force` true
  or a kind string); `lightOn()`; `shroudOn()` (ember only: combat.js actHit and ai.js shroudLook use it). `LIGHT.base`
  = `ZONES[id].ring` on each zone change (6 Act IV, 4 Act V). The swell (`heroLightR`) and `drink()` run in 'ember' only.
- `lightAt(x, z, o)`: beams count; `o.ring === false` leaves out the hero's ring. `fireAt(x, z, pad)` (new export).
- `lampLightAt(x, z)`: lit interactables with lightR except 'holeLamp', and pools added `{ lamp: true }`; never fire or
  beams. `warmAt(x, z)`: lampLightAt, a lit hole lamp, `L.spots.brazier` (r 4).
- `addLightPool` gains `o.decal === false` and `o.lamp`. **The snow fix (B2b's request):** in 'sea' zones a pool's disc is
  rimless, warmer (colour x (1, 0.78, 0.55)) and at alpha 0.13 instead of 0.3, and its light at 0.36 of the asked
  intensity (world.js light5 asks 22 -> 7.9).
- Beams: `addBeam(x, z, o)` -> `{ id, x, z, y, len, half, period, theta, dir, sweep, tag, owner, prop, mesh, pool, light,
  warm, k, heroIn }`, `removeBeam(b | tag)`, `beams()`, `getBeam(tag)`, `setBeamLen(b, d)` (Eclipse), `beamHas(b, x, z)`,
  `inBeam(x, z)` (d in [3, len], |angle - theta| < half + 0.6/d). `o.prop`: an act5Prop with `setBeam` (sealight,
  skerryLight, farLight, markerLight) draws its own cone (build5 beamOn); without a prop, sea.js `beamMesh` (cone: false
  for none). Each frame (tickLight): turns at 2*PI/period, `sweep` counts turns, the pooled light sits where the beam
  meets the ground (0.6 len), the hero is warmed once a pass (`warmHero(4)`, sfx beamHum, bus 'beamPass'), `SEA.uBeam`
  = the nearest beam (origin, k; direction with the cone's dip, len). `SEA.uLights` = the two lit lanterns nearest the
  hero (beams' fires, lit lamps), 4 Hz. `clearLight` drops beams.
- The hero's ring turns white and pulses on webbed/crazed thin ice (stage 2-3), with an `iceCreak` as she steps on it
  (design "danger at the hero's feet").

### src/game/sap.js

  (`f.oil`), bus 'oilBurst', a cask within 3.1 m goes up 0.45 s later. ice.js melts under fire areas (+1/2 s, B1b).

### src/game/combat.js
- `act4Hit` -> `actHit`, which ends `return k * vuln(a)`. **The vulnerability rule:** `VULN` table (revealed/exposed =
  `HAZ5.reveal`, dazed/beached/stranded/seared 1.5, blinded 1.6, floundering/overturned 2); `vuln(a)` = max of `a.vulns`
  and a daze's `dmgTaken > 1`, capped 1.6 for a boss unless `a.vulnWindow`; `addVuln(a, kind, t, o)` (hit text once
  per 0.8 s, `o.quiet`; a rim colour while it lasts; floundering never on a boss); `vulnOf(a)` -> `{ kind, k, t }` for the
  boss bar; `clearVuln(a, kind)`; ticked in `tickStatus`. `dmgTaken < 1` and `a.resist` multiply apart.
- `hurtHero`: `o.cold` -> coldAdd (after iframes); Skerry's Shell: `o.plunge || o.wash` costs no life.
- `rootHero(t, o)`: `o.tint` -> `status.rootTint` (ice-blue particles, sfx frost); `freeHero`/tickStatus crack it in ice
  colours; player.js tints her by `rootTint`.
- `moveMul`: x `coldMul()` for the hero.
- `kill`: oil casks burst; `o.drown` -> `a.drowned` (no corpse, no decal, splash; ai.js dying sinks it 1.8 s); flesh
  'drowned' (hitFlesh, splash death with kelp bits) and 'skotos' (hitSpirit, black mist); a boss's first-kill legendary is
  `LEG_FROM[a.kind]`; Einar's Lantern reveals unlit foes it strikes (2 s).

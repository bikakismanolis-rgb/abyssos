## What I built

### audio.js
- **INS.iceSong(v, t, o)**: the ice's song: a sine falling exponentially ~3 kHz -> ~400 Hz in 0.25-0.6 s, a dispersed
  second partial, an onset click, a second ping (o.ping chance), the long tail from the reverb. Used by the sfx, stings
  and every Act V theme.
- **SFX (50 new)**, the contract's 26: tideBell (low bell on D/A, detuned, rung three times), surfSwell, washOut, splash,
  plunge, iceCreak, iceCrack1, iceCrack2, iceCrack3 (the 0.6 s warning, loud), iceBreak, refreeze, iceSing, harpoonThrow,
  harpoonReel, sealBark, sealLunge, skuaCry, louseSkitter, louseCurl, louseRoll, oilCask, beamHum, lightCatch,
  freezeWave, skotosRise, towerBell (a bell with a partial out of true; pitch moves it). Extras for combat and story:
  tideTurn, slush, hook (the boat-hook), icemawSurface, icemawDive, frostRoar (the Rime Bear), singerSong, singerWail,
  beamBell (each turn of a beam), lampSnuff, gustRise (3 s spindrift before a gust), blizzard (the 8 s howl), towerBreach,
  towerDive, brineSpit, skotosInhale (1.2 s: start it with the Black Breath's telegraph), skotosBreath (the exhale),
  skotosSweep, skotosSlam, skotosRoar, skotosDrink, handRise, handLash (a lash with the ice shrieking), smother.
  LIMITS and TRIM for each (levels from the offline probe against the shipped families).
- **STINGS**: sealight (the sea-light motif D A G D' on soft brass in fifths + flute, a major sixth blooming under its
  last note), naming (the title's D minor, low, swelling), relight (the lantern motif on flute and the sea-light motif on
  brass together, resolving to D major with choir and taiko), memoryIce (Ice Memories and Name-stones). STING_TRIM set.
- **SEA_MOTIF** [[62,1.5],[69,.5],[67,.5],[74,2.5]] and seaMotif(th, t, beat, o) (flute, optional brass in fifths, tr);
  forgetting(th, t, beat, pi) (MAIN on a low sine, each note dropped with MOOD.forget); surf() (an ~8 s wave following
  the tide), gull(), swell() (cymbal), crackedBell(); DRUMS.tower (6/8 on 12 cells).
- **THEMES**: coast (54 bpm 6/8 D dorian; drone D/A, strings, surf by MOOD.tide, ice song 6-14 s, faster with MOOD.ice,
  glass bells with G# under the green sky; fight: taiko every 2nd bar + ostinato; MOOD.bloom: a major-sixth chord; sky 1:
  bells off, surf to a hiss, whisper, the forgetting; sky 2: D major, sea-light motif every 16 bars, gulls), farlight
  (48 bpm no beat E aeolian; wind, frequent ice song, forgetting; E1 sub drone growing with MOOD.skotos; sky 2: E major,
  bells, choir, slow string line, the motif), tower (6/8 112 bpm D phrygian, DRUMS.tower; horn fifths with the phrygian
  answer, cracked bell every 4th bar; phase 0 surf + a taiko/brass hit on each Breach (MOOD.sub 1->0); phase 1 no drums,
  ice clicks, choir 'u', ice song; phase 2 with MOOD.lit the motif blaring on brass over a high choir, drums back when
  it gutters), skotos (5/4 92 bpm E phrygian, brass ostinato E/F 3+2, choir 'u', drums on 1 and 4; phase 1 the Night:
  heartbeat + sub drone + the forgetting, a double thump on each MOOD.under rising edge; phase 2 dark: near silence; phase
  2 lit: E major, both motifs together over E5, full choir/strings/brass, a cymbal swell per MOOD.sear), sealit (60 bpm D
  major: the motif harmonised on brass + flute, answered by the lantern motif; MOOD.answer: a bell and the ANSWER phrase;
  MOOD.hold: a held D major chord).
- **MOOD** gains tide, ice, forget (0-0.3), skotos, under, lit (bool), sky (0/1/2), gust, sub, freeze, and the moments
  bloom, sear, hold (MOMENTS are cleared on every theme change; outside the sea's themes freeze/skotos/sub/gust/under are
  cleared too).
- **Graph**: music path duck -> g.mlp (low-pass) -> g.mvol -> master (wet path through g.mlpW); master path masterIn ->
  g.flp (low-pass) -> compressor. applyMood (every scheduler tick): music low-pass toward 500 Hz with MOOD.skotos on
  'farlight', 500 Hz while MOOD.sub on 'tower', 650 Hz in the Skotos's Night; mvol 0.7 while MOOD.gust (the howl's 30%
  duck); master low-pass toward 300 Hz with MOOD.freeze (the Freeze: music and sound).

### hud.js
- **Cold meter** #cold in the amber slot (Act V zones; amber hidden there): snowflake (SNOW, built in code), a blue
  fill marked at 50 and 75, hud.cold/chilled/freezing/frostbite, an arrow while it climbs/falls (COLD.dir), Freezing glows,
  Frostbite pulses, a warm tint while Warmed. **Warmed** is a flame buff (hud.warmed title).
- **Tide dial** #tide at the minimap's lower left (#mmw wrapper): the ring counts down to the next turn (tideDial().next /
  the phase's length), gold and pulsing while the bell rings, red with the lane's 6 s when the water is closing round her
  (and a 'warn' toast hud.lane once + a high bell), dark while a boss owns the water; the water level inside; arrow up/down,
  a bar at slack; title hud.tide · hud.tide.<phase>. Shown only in play in a zone with a tide.
- **First-time hints** (h.flags.hints): hint.cold (first Chilled), hint.tide (first bell), hint.ice (first stage-2 cell
  under her, polled 4 Hz), hint.louse (bus 'louseCurl'); 'hint' toasts last 6 s. **q.tideTurn** once per visit at the
  first bell.
- **Sounds that carry timing**: tideTurn sfx at the flood and ebb starts; gustRise on 'gustWarn'; blizzard + MOOD.gust on
  'gust'; beamBell on 'beamTurn' (positional); lampSnuff on 'lampSnuffed' (Act V zones).
- **Boss music**: OWN_THEME (amaranthe, ivar, karthax, tower, skotos): bossIntro resets phase/lit/sub/under and plays the
  boss's theme; bossPhase -> MOOD.phase; bossDown of the story Skotos stops the music (the names' bells and the naming's
  chord, then story's 'sealit'); other bosses go back to ZONES[id].music + victory. Boss toast in Greek capitals without
  accents (ΤΟ ΣΚΟΤΟΣ). **The boss's open window** under its bar: combat's vulnOf(a) as «Αναποδογύρισε! ×2» /
  "Overturned! ×2" (pulsing; brighter from ×1.6).
- zoneEnter resets the sea's filter moods; minimap: Act V ice repaints once a second, actors `a.under` hidden.

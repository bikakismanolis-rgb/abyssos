## Findings

1. **tide.js:94 / ice.js:81, TIDE and ICE stale after leaving the coast: fixed.** Reproduced with `fix/bind.mjs`: after coast → town with the waypoints panel open, `TIDE` was still set 1.5 s later, `tideDial()` returned phase 'low', and `ICE` was still set. Fix: world.js `leaveZone` calls `resetTide(null)` and `resetIce(null)`, which already null their bindings for a zone without `L.bed` / `L.ice`. act5Enter binds them again. After the fix: TIDE null, dial null, ICE null, both coast → town and farlight → town.
2. **gen5.js:655, freezeBay slivers flagged as frozen.pocket: fixed.** Reproduced: with the pre-fix gen5 (`GEN5=fix/old/...`), `check-act5 400 777` exits 1 with frozen.pocket on seeds 7797741 and 15316242. pocket.mjs shows 2-3 cell columns at x = C.x − 24. Fix: freezeBay records the cells it freezes, floods from the ice road over thin ice (4-connected), and turns every frozen cell the flood does not reach back to water (cells 0, low 1, ice 0). After the fix: `fix/icecomp.mjs` finds 0 cut-off cells over 400 seeds, and pocket.mjs is clean on all four reported seeds.
3. **gen5.js:255/470/962, Name-stones and door-stone on open cells: fixed.** Reproduced with `fix/stones.mjs`: the carriers and sailor stones stood on open cells in 60/60 seeds, and the farlight door cell was open in 60/60. Fix: `blockC(st.x, st.z, 0.45)` for carriers and sailor, the same as keyx and thaleia, and `blockC` on the door-stone's cell (cx−1, cz+2). The door cell is not added to `farLight.cells`, because check-act5 requires 9 there. Its interact point at d.z − 0.4 is the open cell north of it. Footprints make the carriers cell BED_DRY, so the tide never opens it. After the fix: 0/60 and 0/60, and check-act5 is clean, including reach.stone, reach.belly, reach.arena and core.notByThick.
4. **panels.js:222, the Act I panel advertises Skotos on Ash: fixed.** Confirmed in the code: `act()` showed `DIFFS[h.diff + 1]` without checking it. Fix: it shows the next difficulty only when it exists and `diffUnlocked(h.diff + 1)` is true. `fix/actpanel.mjs` gives Wanderer → Warden, Warden → Hero, Hero → Nightmare, Nightmare → Ash, Ash → none, Skotos → none (no crash at index 6). Note: `act()` is outside B1's part of panels.js (diffUnlocked and the waypoints). It is a DIFFS consumer that owner decision 2 requires to handle index 5. The change is one line.
5. **sea.js:460/497, the see-through sea drawn over every renderOrder-0 transparent (major): fixed.** Reproduced: the reviewer's halo.mjs shot, and `fix/haloro.mjs`. With the sea at renderOrder 1, a fire halo in front of closed water adds 7.6 RGB, against 141 with no sea behind it (ratio 0.05). Fix: `SEA_RO = -0.6` for the coast's transparent chunks and the far water. The opaque leads (−0.5) and the ice (−1) sort in the opaque list, so they are unaffected. The ice windows (1), blob shadows (1.5), pools, rings and decals (2) and telegraphs (3) still draw after it. After the fix: ratio 1.01, and halo.mjs's ro0 shot now matches the reviewer's ro5 shot (`fix/halo/halo-ro0.png`). shallowtele gains a halo case: a flameMesh halo 1.2 m over closed water, read with and without the sea, pass > 0.75. It reads 0.05 under the old order and 0.98 / 1.01 now. **Contract deviation:** the contract's "water 1" is now −0.6; the order the contract lists is kept.
6. **world.js:281, disposeZone leaks z.extra geometry: fixed.** Reproduced with the reviewer's leak.mjs: geometries went 208 → 270 → 332 → 394. Fix: disposeZone traverses the level and z.extra. For meshes, lines and points it disposes the geometry unless `userData.shared`, and it calls `InstancedMesh.dispose()`. It disposes the materials marked `userData.own`: build.js gains `own()`, and `glowMat` uses it, as do flameMesh's halo, build5's halo, lantern glass, cask stain and drowned-lantern sprite, and sea.js's beam material. It also drops sea.js TICKS entries whose prop was disposed, since a lit beam kept its prop alive. Textures are never disposed, because phones release the decoded image once it is uploaded. After the fix: 141 geometries every cycle (programs 65-66, against 69-71 before). `fix/rebuild.mjs` (diffChanged and the Freeze's rebuild) draws the towers, windows and ground again with no errors (`fix/rb/*.png`).
7. **build.js:1342, G_WET discards only past 0.7 m, and low water is not gated: partly confirmed, fixed.** Confirmed: perf5 measured only h 1.2, and low water is slower. The stated cause does not hold at perf5's spot. `fix/discard.mjs` shows that at h 0 the screen there is 100% dry flats, ground that has to be drawn, and none of it is water. At h 1.2, 29% of the screen is past 0.7 m and 11.5% is in the 0.5-0.7 m band. Low water costs more because exposed sand replaces cheap water, not because of a hidden sea floor. Fixes anyway:
   - The discard is now past 0.5 m. The sea is at alpha 0.95 from 0.43 m, and the water's corner beds are never above the ground's (the ground counts thick, window and deck cells as dry), so wherever the ground is cut the water over it is already 95% opaque. A pixel A/B of 0.7 against 0.5 (a scratch copy on :5362, now stopped) is within the noise floor of the same build rendered twice.
   - perf5 gains a `coastLow` visit: the same Shallows spot at tide 0, gated like the others.
8. **sea.js:952/1014, pow() with a negative base: fixed.** Confirmed: on this GL `1 − |dot(v, v)|` for a normalized v comes out below 0 (`fix/pownan.mjs`). Swiftshader's pow returns no NaN there, so the specks cannot be reproduced here. GLSL leaves pow undefined for a negative base. Fix: `pow(max(1.0 − |dot|, 0.0), k)` in the beam cone and in skotosSkin's fresnel. (build.js:1865, Act III's amber lake `pow(1.0 − V.y, 3.0)`, has the same pattern. It is left alone because it is outside Act V and the play camera never looks straight down.)
9. **fx.js:239, decals float on the water: fixed.** Confirmed in the code: a decal kept full opacity at renderOrder 2 whatever water lay over it. Fix: decal() reads the bed under the stain from FX.L5 (bilinear on L.vbed, like the sea's depth) and fades it by what the sea's alpha leaves at that depth, using FX.tideLv, or FX.bayLv in Skerry Bay (sea.js now writes FX.bayLv and FX.iceSt). It is hidden once the water is closed. No decal is made on an L.sea cell. On thin ice, the stain goes when its cell breaks (stage 4). Measured with `fix/decal.mjs`: a stain on a 0.45 m bed flat reads 0.75 at low water, 0.38 under 0.26 m of water, and is hidden at 1.15. A stain on dry land stays at 0.75 (`fix/dc/*.png`).
